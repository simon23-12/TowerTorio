How to Play

⚔ The Corridor

Enemies pour out of a long, pitch-black corridor toward your Core. Only Sector 1 is visible — and only through fog.

Click inside a sector to build its Watchtower — where in the sector doesn't matter, it covers the whole sector. Only the front tower is active. Beat its sector's 3 waves to clear it: enemies stop, the next sector lights up and the fog moves on. Build in the next sector to advance — the old tower shuts down for good. Towers identify the enemies they see.

Your starting tower is pathetic. It will fall within the first few waves unless you power it up.

Kills earn ◈ Credits.

⚙ The Factory

A separate map. Press Tab to switch. Place Quarry Drills on ore, belt the ore into Refineries, then belt the crystals into a Teleporter. You can buy more teleporters. V shows both maps side by side.

Each resource powers one stat: Alcamanium → Attack, Vitrium → HP, Quicksilite → Dexterity, Voltarite → Energy.

Tiers depend on the steady flow (crystals/min). Ore never runs out — but if supply drops, so does your tower.

Spend credits on new machines, Upgrades (every building of a type at once) and Research (new elements).

Enemy Codex

Enemies are identified once they enter a tower's clear vision.

Upgrades

Each upgrade improves every building of that type — the ones you have and the ones you build later.

Research

Unlock new elements to quarry. Each one powers a different tower stat.
